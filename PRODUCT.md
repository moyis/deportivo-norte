# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Marplatenses with a tie to Club Deportivo Norte, a 1937 neighborhood football club in the La Perla barrio of Mar del Plata, Argentina. Three audiences:

- **Prospective members** ("socios") deciding whether to join. The primary conversion: fill the membership form or visit the predio.
- **Fans and families** of the barrio, "La 90" hinchada, parents looking to enroll kids in inferiores.
- **Discovery traffic**: people searching "clubes de fútbol de Mar del Plata" who land here cold and need to grasp what the club is in seconds.

Context of use: mostly mobile, in Spanish (Argentina, "vos" conjugation), often a quick visit triggered by social media or a search.

## Product Purpose

A single-page landing site that turns interest into membership and pride. It exists to (1) convert visitors into socios, (2) make the club's 1937 history and identity legible and emotional, and (3) win local search visibility for Mar del Plata football queries. Success = membership form submissions and a site that feels unmistakably like *this* club, not a template.

## Brand Personality

Raw barrio passion. Working-class football identity, loud and emotional, heart over polish. Three words: **passionate, proud, neighborhood**. Voice is warm and direct in Argentine Spanish ("sumate", "viví la pasión"). The yellow-and-black is hinchada energy, not a corporate palette. The emotional goal is belonging: a visitor should feel the pull of joining a family with 88 years of history, not read a brochure.

## Anti-references

This must NOT look like:

- **Generic AI landing**: cream/warm-neutral backgrounds, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, the hero-metric template. The 2026 slop scaffold.
- **Corporate SaaS**: clinical blue/gray, startup-template sterility. Too cold for a football club.
- **Big-club clone**: mimicking Boca / River / Premier League sites. Borrowed prestige the club hasn't earned; it should look like what it is, a proud barrio institution.
- **Cluttered news portal**: ad-heavy, sidebar-stuffed, busy old-school club site.

## Design Principles

1. **Heart over polish.** Emotion and identity beat slickness. If a choice is technically clean but feels corporate, it's wrong.
2. **Own the yellow.** Club colors are the voice. Commit to yellow/black; don't hedge with neutral grays around the edges.
3. **History is the hook.** 1937, the bicampeonato, Nora Vega, "La 90". Real specifics, real names, real quotes carry the page, not stock-photo generality.
4. **One job per fold.** The page sells membership. Every section should funnel toward asociarse without burying the CTA.
5. **Barrio, not brochure.** Argentine Spanish, real people, real predio. Distinctive beats safe; a template is invisible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. The codebase already ships skip links, ARIA labels, focus-visible outlines, and reduced-motion considerations should be honored on the carousel and bounce animations. Yellow-on-black and black-on-yellow contrast must stay AA for all text. Spanish (es-AR) throughout.
